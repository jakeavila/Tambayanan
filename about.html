About the concept

Vacant time,
better spent.

Tambayanan is a student tambayanan concept designed around a simple idea: students deserve a comfortable place to spend the time between classes.

Our idea

A place for both people and peace.

Sometimes students want to relax and talk with friends. Other times, they need a quiet place to finish schoolwork. Tambayanan brings both options together while keeping the social area separate from the quiet study room.

Comfort first

A welcoming place to rest between classes.

Space to focus

A separate quiet room for study time.

Student-friendly

Simple amenities and proposed hourly pricing.
